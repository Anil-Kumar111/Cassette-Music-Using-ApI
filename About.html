Cassette is a modern and lightweight music streaming web application developed using the JioSaavn API. It provides users access to a massive collection of songs, albums, artists, and playlists directly from JioSaavn's extensive library. Designed with a clean and minimalistic UI featuring , Cassette offers an intuitive and smooth listening experience. Users can search for any track in real time, browse trending music, and instantly stream high-quality audio through seamless playback controls like play, pause, seek, and volume adjustment.
By harnessing the power of the JioSaavn API, Cassette ensures a fast, reliable, and enjoyable streaming platform that feels fresh, modern, and nostalgic all at once.
